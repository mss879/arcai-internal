## 4. Things worth checking

Observations from the final numbers. Questions, not recommendations.

### A. The Rs 175,000 collision is the sharpest one

Smart Site (15-page website + CRM + AI answers) and the WhatsApp Agent + CRM are both Rs 175,000. A prospect who only wants WhatsApp will notice that the same money buys them a whole website on top. That's either a great upsell lever or it makes the standalone agent look overpriced — worth deciding which story you tell, because the agent will be asked.

### B. Instagram is cheaper than WhatsApp by Rs 25,000

Same product, different channel. If the build effort is genuinely similar, be ready for "why is Instagram cheaper?" — and note a client could buy the Instagram agent at Rs 150,000 as the cheaper way in.

### C. Ladder steps are now clean

Rs 175k → 250k → 450k. The Rs 75,000 step to Smart Business buys the action-taking agent and advanced CRM; the Rs 200,000 step to Smart System buys two more channels, auto follow-up, advanced SEO and 3 automations. The top step is large — Smart System being "from" gives room to scope up, but it also means the flagship is nearly 2× Smart Business, which is where prospects tend to stall.

### D. Zero recurring revenue on everything current

Every current package is one-time. The only recurring lines left are Website Protection, Monthly SEO, the client's own AI usage, and Social Media retainers. "You own it, no rent" is a genuinely strong closing angle in this market — just be clear it's deliberate, because it caps lifetime value and means growth depends entirely on new deals.

### E. The legacy AI tiers now contradict the new lineup

Flow (Rs 75k + 10k/mo), Engage (Rs 135k + 15k/mo), Qualify (Rs 195k + 24k/mo) and Command (Rs 310k + 45k/mo) were never repriced and still carry retainers. **Engage at Rs 135,000 + Rs 15,000/month is a website chat assistant — Smart Site at Rs 175,000 includes a website, a CRM and an agent with no monthly fee.** Engage is now the worse deal on almost every axis, and Command (Rs 310k + 45k/mo) is undercut by Smart System. These four sit in the same catalog the agent quotes from, so a customer can be shown both. They need repricing, retiring, or clear positioning as something different.

### F. E-Commerce Store sits oddly against Smart Site

Both start at very different points now — the store is from Rs 150,000 with no CRM or agent, while Rs 175,000 gets a full website plus both. If a store client asks "why would I not just take Smart Site?", the honest answer is that they're different products, but the price gap of Rs 25,000 makes it a fair question.

### G. Smart Store layer still costs more than the store itself

Store from Rs 150,000; adding the automation layer from Rs 200,000. The maths is internally consistent (Rs 350,000 either way), but "the add-on costs more than the thing" needs a confident explanation on a call.

### H. Extra pages vs the top tier

Rs 6,000/page. Going 25 → 50 pages is Rs 150,000 in page fees alone, while Smart Business → Smart System is Rs 200,000 and also adds Instagram, WhatsApp follow-up, advanced SEO and 3 automations. Smart System therefore reads as strong value, which helps upsell — just confirm it stays profitable at the 50-page ceiling.
