## 2. Core Capabilities & Feature Breakdown

---

### 🌟 1. The 24/7 WhatsApp AI Sales Representative
*An autonomous sales expert living directly inside your business WhatsApp number.*

- **Instant 24/7 Responses**: Greets every customer the exact second they text—day or night, weekends or holidays.
- **Speaks the Customer's Language**: Contextually understands and replies in **English**, **Sinhala**, or **Tamil** (including conversational text like *Singlish* and *Tanglish*).
- **Performs Live Website Audits in Chat**: If a customer provides their website link, the AI audits their site live in seconds, pointing out speed issues and mobile flaws to prove your expertise on the spot.
- **Sends Visual Redesign Concepts**: Sends custom visual redesign mockups directly into the WhatsApp conversation to excite the prospect.
- **Honors Customer Promises**: If a client says, *"I'm busy today, message me Monday at 9 AM"* or *"My budget gets approved on the 25th"*, the AI automatically pauses standard messages and reaches out at that exact promised moment.
- **Direct Appointment Booking**: Agrees on a convenient phone call time in the chat and places it on your team's calendar with reminder alerts.
- **Dispatches Digital Quotes & Price Lists**: Generates signable quotes and branded price list documents on request.
- **Seamless Human Handoff**: Flags conversations that need personal attention and instantly notifies your team to step in when needed.

---

### 🎯 2. Automated Lead Hunter & Technical Radar
*Find new clients in any city and industry without spending days manually searching.*

- **Local Business Discovery**: Enter any industry and location (e.g., *"Restaurants in Colombo"*, *"Real Estate in Kandy"*) to scan real operating businesses.
- **Automatic Digital Flaw Detection**: Analyzes each business to find:
  - Businesses with **no website at all**
  - Businesses with **broken or slow mobile websites**
  - Outdated copyright years and poor search engine rankings
- **Ready-to-Send Personalized Outreach**: Automatically crafts personalized emails and text messages pointing out the specific problems found, giving you a warm opening pitch.
- **Automatic Duplicate Prevention**: Ensures you never contact the same business twice or message someone already in your active pipeline.

---

### 📊 3. Visual CRM & Lead Intelligence
*A clean, visual pipeline where every deal is tracked from first hello to final payment.*

- **Drag-and-Drop Deal Board**: Move leads effortlessly across customized stages (*New Lead $\to$ Contacted $\to$ Proposal Sent $\to$ In Negotiation $\to$ Won*).
- **AI Lead Scoring**: Highlights **HOT**, **WARM**, and **COLD** leads so your team always knows who is most ready to buy right now.
- **AI Next-Best-Action Guide**: Tells your team exactly what step to take next for each lead (*e.g., "No contact for 5 days — send project follow-up"*).
- **One-Click Client History**: View every text message, email, meeting note, quote, and payment tied to a customer in one single profile.

---

### ⚡ 4. Visual Workflow Automations
*Put your business communication on autopilot.*

- **Visual Workflow Builder**: Create multi-step automations without any coding. Connect triggers (*e.g., Lead Created, Stage Moved, Quote Opened*) to actions.
- **Multi-Channel Engagement**:
  - Send automated SMS and WhatsApp messages
  - Send branded confirmation emails
  - Assign follow-up tasks to staff members with strict deadlines
- **Pre-Built Automation Recipes**:
  - **New Lead Keep-Warm Flow**: Welcomes new inquiries and sends structured follow-ups over several days so leads never go cold.
  - **Instant Form Response**: Immediately texts anyone who submits an inquiry on your website while alerting your entire team.
  - **Unpaid Invoice Reminders**: Gently alerts clients before and after payment due dates automatically.

---

### ✍️ 5. Instant E-Sign Proposals & Deal Closer
*Turn interested conversations into signed contracts in minutes.*

- **Interactive Digital Quotations**: Create clean, professional proposals that clients can open on their phone or computer via a private link.
- **Live Viewing Alerts**: The instant a client opens your quote, ARC OS alerts your team and prepares your WhatsApp AI to follow up while they are actively reviewing it.
- **Mobile Digital Signature**: Clients can sign with their finger on mobile or mouse on desktop in seconds.
- **Autonomous Post-Signing Actions**: The second a client signs:
  1. The deal moves automatically to **Won**
  2. A new **Project** is created with all milestones
  3. The first **Deposit Invoice** is generated with your company seal
  4. Bank payment instructions are dispatched immediately
  5. Your onboarding team is alerted with their kickoff checklist

### 💳 6. Financial Operations, Invoices & Cash Flow
*Get paid faster and maintain complete financial clarity.*

- **Branded PDF Invoices with Company Seal**: Professional, itemized invoices ready to download or email directly with one click.
- **Milestone & Installment Tracking**: Easily manage phased payment structures (*e.g., 50% Advance $\to$ 25% Milestone $\to$ 25% Completion*).
- **Post-Dated Cheque Tracker**: Logs cheque numbers, bank branches, and maturity dates, sending proactive reminders before they are due.
- **Expense & Profit Tracking**: Record overhead and project expenses to see real-time profit and loss figures.
- **Team Commission Management**: Allocate project commissions to team members; each staff member tracks their own earned balance on their private dashboard.
