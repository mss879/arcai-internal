# Video Prompt — "Meet Arc: The WhatsApp AI Sales Agent"

Promo video for the ARC AI WhatsApp sales agent. Written for AI video generators (Veo, Sora, Kling, Motion, etc.). Each scene is a self-contained prompt — paste the **Global Style Block** together with each scene when your tool generates clips one at a time.

**Target length:** ~90 seconds (14 scenes × ~6–8s) · **Aspect:** 16:9 master (also render 9:16 for WhatsApp/Reels/TikTok)

---

## Global Style Block (prepend to every scene prompt)

> Sleek modern tech promo, premium motion-graphics style mixed with photoreal close-ups of a smartphone running a WhatsApp-style chat interface. Color palette: WhatsApp green (#25D366), deep charcoal (#0F1B22), clean white, soft mint accents. Smooth camera moves, shallow depth of field, soft studio lighting with green rim light. UI elements animate crisply (messages pop in with subtle spring, typing indicator pulses). Sri Lankan business setting: warm, authentic, optimistic. No robots, no sci-fi clichés — the AI feels invisible and human. Modern minimal sans-serif for on-screen text. Music: confident, upbeat electronic groove that builds throughout. Voiceover: warm, confident Sri Lankan-accented English, conversational, slightly playful.

---

## Scene 1 — The Problem (Hook)
- **Visual:** Night. A small bakery, lights off. A phone on the counter lights up with WhatsApp notifications stacking up: "Do you deliver?", "Price for a website?", a voice note. No one answers. Notifications fade to "seen tomorrow". Clock spins from 11 PM to 9 AM.
- **On-screen text:** "Every missed message is a missed sale."
- **VO:** "Your customers message you at eleven at night. And by morning… they've bought from someone else."
- **SFX:** Muffled notification pings, low moody pad before the beat drops.

## Scene 2 — Meet Arc
- **Visual:** Beat drops. The same phone flips over in daylight. A new inquiry arrives — instantly two blue ticks appear, then "typing…" pulses, and a warm reply pops in seconds later. Camera pushes into the screen; a subtle green pulse radiates from the phone.
- **On-screen text:** "Meet Arc — your AI sales agent on WhatsApp."
- **VO:** "Meet Arc. An AI salesperson living inside your business WhatsApp — answering every customer in moments, twenty-four seven."

## Scene 3 — Talks Like a Human, in 3 Languages
- **Visual:** Three phones slide in side by side, same conversation flowing in English, Sinhala, and Tamil. Messages are short, warm, human — with a single emoji. A "bot-speak" message ("Dear valued customer…") gets stamped with a red X and shatters.
- **On-screen text:** "Short. Warm. Human. English · සිංහල · தமிழ்"
- **VO:** "It texts like your best salesperson — short, friendly, human — in English, Sinhala or Tamil. Never like a bot."

## Scene 4 — Hears Voice Notes, Answers Back
- **Visual:** Close-up: a customer records a voice note while driving. On Arc's side the waveform morphs into text, then Arc replies with its own voice note — animated green waveform speaking back.
- **On-screen text:** "Understands voice notes. Replies with its own."
- **VO:** "Customers who talk instead of type? Arc listens to voice notes — and can even reply with its own voice."

## Scene 5 — Sees Photos & Payment Slips
- **Visual:** A customer snaps a bank transfer slip and sends it. On screen, glowing scan-lines sweep the slip; amount, bank, and reference highlight in green. Cut to a manager's phone buzzing: "💰 Payment slip received — verify now" with a task card sliding in.
- **On-screen text:** "Reads payment slips. Alerts your team instantly. Humans confirm the money."
- **VO:** "Send it a photo — even a bank slip. Arc reads it, thanks the customer, and instantly tasks your team to verify. It never confirms money on its own."

## Scene 6 — The CRM That Fills Itself
- **Visual:** Split screen. Left: the chat ("I'm Nimal, from Nimal Bakery"). Right: a CRM pipeline board where a lead card materializes — name, business, phone — then slides into the pipeline, gets a flame icon ("HOT") and a deal value. Team phones light up: "New WhatsApp lead 🔥".
- **On-screen text:** "Every chat becomes a lead. Automatically."
- **VO:** "The moment someone shares their name, Arc creates the lead, logs every detail, scores it hot or cold — and pings your whole team. Zero data entry."

## Scene 7 — Instant Website Audit
- **Visual:** Customer sends "our site is nimalbakery.lk". A radial gauge spins up and lands on 34/100 in red; icons flag "slow on mobile", "not secure". Back in chat, Arc casually mentions the real numbers.
- **On-screen text:** "Audits their website — live, mid-chat."
- **VO:** "Share a website, and seconds later Arc has tested it — real Google speed scores, real issues — and talks about it like an expert who just browsed the site."

## Scene 8 — Silent Deep Research
- **Visual:** Behind the chat, a translucent layer shows threads of light connecting reviews, a map pin, competitor logos, a Facebook page — weaving into a glowing dossier. In the chat, Arc drops a knowing insight. The customer replies "wow, you know your stuff 😅".
- **On-screen text:** "It does its homework. Silently."
- **VO:** "While chatting, Arc quietly researches their business — reviews, reputation, competitors — then just… knows things. Customers feel it instantly."

## Scene 9 — The Showcase (Show, Don't Tell)
- **Visual:** Hero moment. An image message lands in the chat: a before/after — the customer's tired old homepage morphing into a stunning AI-generated redesign concept, with audit scores beside it. The customer's eyes widen; they forward it to a partner.
- **On-screen text:** "Their own website — reimagined. Delivered in the chat."
- **VO:** "Then the closer: a personalised page with their audit scores… and an AI redesign of their own homepage. Arc doesn't tell customers what you could do — it shows them."

## Scene 10 — Sells From Your Playbook
- **Visual:** A clean package card appears in chat — "*Launch — Rs 90,000*" with ticked inclusions — then the next tier slides in beside it. A calendar booking page opens with a tap. A proposal document assembles itself from chat bubbles.
- **On-screen text:** "Your packages. Your prices. Your rules."
- **VO:** "It sells from your price list — never invents a number — recommends the right package, books meetings, and even drafts full proposals from the conversation."

## Scene 11 — Closed With a Signature
- **Visual:** Customer texts "okay let's do it 👍". A formal quotation card drops into the chat. Finger signs on the phone screen — signature ink flows — then a chain reaction animates: invoice ✓, payment details ✓, deposit task ✓, lead marked WON. Green confetti, restrained.
- **On-screen text:** "Yes → signed → invoiced. Automatically."
- **VO:** "And when they say yes — a signable quotation lands right in the chat. One signature on their phone, and the invoice, payment details and kickoff happen by themselves."

## Scene 12 — Never Lets a Deal Go Quiet
- **Visual:** A timeline stretches across the screen: Day 2 — gentle nudge message; Day 5 — a voice-note follow-up plays; Day 10 — a graceful goodbye. A moon icon shows nudges waiting politely until morning. The instant the customer replies, all scheduled bubbles dissolve.
- **On-screen text:** "Follows up until there's an answer. Stops the second they reply."
- **VO:** "If a lead goes quiet, Arc follows up — three smart touches, even a voice note — never at 2 a.m., and it stops the instant they reply."

## Scene 13 — You Stay in Control
- **Visual:** The team inbox dashboard: conversations list, an "AI paused — human takeover" toggle flips as a teammate types; a wall of permission switches (Quotes ✓, Research ✓, Discounts ✗); an activity log scrolls every action with timestamps. A "STOP" message from a customer instantly greys out all automation.
- **On-screen text:** "Human takeover, one tap. Every action logged. Stop always means stop."
- **VO:** "Your team watches every chat, takes over anytime, and decides exactly what Arc is allowed to do. Every action, logged. Every opt-out, honoured — instantly."

## Scene 14 — Gets Smarter + CTA
- **Visual:** A weekly report card flips through coaching notes; a performance line curves upward week over week. Cut to black; the ARC AI wordmark draws in with a green pulse, the phone from Scene 1 now glowing with happy conversations.
- **On-screen text:** "It reviews its own deals and gets better every week." → "ARC AI — arc.ai.agencylk@gmail.com" → "Want Arc on your number? Message us — Arc will answer."
- **VO:** "Every week, Arc studies its own conversations and coaches itself to sell better. Want it on your WhatsApp number? Message ARC AI — and let Arc answer you first."

- **Reusable characters:** "Nimal / Nimal Bakery" runs through Scenes 6–11 for narrative continuity.
- **Cutdowns:** 30s version = Scenes 1, 2, 7, 9, 11, 14. 15s teaser = Scenes 1, 9, 11, 14.
- **Localized VO:** record Sinhala and Tamil VO variants over the same visuals for local campaigns.
