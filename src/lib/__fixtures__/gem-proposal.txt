01 OVERVIEW
Sri Lanka's gem trade runs on relationships and physical inspection. Buyers come to Colombo or travel up to Ratnapura, sit with a
dealer, and judge a stone in daylight. That works beautifully in person and almost not at all online, which is why most gem businesses
here are still selling through WhatsApp photographs and a catalogue that lives in someone's head.
This proposal covers a complete, custom-built website for your gem business: a storefront with a proper stone catalogue, a structured
sourcing request system, an interactive map that lets a buyer choose where in Sri Lanka their stones are sourced from, and a secure
payment gateway so a sale closes on the site rather than across three channels.
It is built as a real application — a fast public front end and a full back end you log into — not a template with a contact form bolted on.
Every enquiry, every stone, every order and every customer lands in one place that you control.
02 OBJECTIVES
Commercial
• Turn the website into a channel that closes sales, not just one that collects enquiries.
• Accept deposits and full payments online, in local and international currency, through a secure gateway.
• Capture qualified sourcing briefs from buyers abroad who cannot fly in to view stones.
• Present the inventory in a way that stands up next to international gem dealers online.

Operational

03 KEY FEATURES
Gem Catalogue & Storefront
The public face of the business — a fast, image-led catalogue built for stones rather than for generic products.
• Individual stone pages with high-resolution gallery, video support, and a zoom view that does justice to colour and clarity.
• Structured gem attributes: type, carat weight, cut, colour, clarity, treatment, origin, certification body and certificate number.
• Filter and search by any attribute — a buyer looking for an unheated 3–5ct Ceylon blue sapphire finds it in two clicks.
• Availability states: available, on hold, reserved and sold, so the catalogue never sells the same stone twice.
• Collections and featured sets for curating parcels, new arrivals or a signature range.

04 THE INTERACTIVE SOURCING MAP
This is the piece that will set the site apart, and it is included in the base price. Instead of a dropdown asking a buyer to pick a city, the
sourcing journey happens on an interactive, animated model of Sri Lanka.
How It Works
• A stylised three-dimensional map of the island sits at the centre of the sourcing page — no plugin, no download.
• Sourcing regions are marked as glowing points — Colombo and Ratnapura first, with room to add Beruwala, Elahera or Balangoda.
• Hovering a region lifts and highlights it; the surrounding terrain settles back so attention goes exactly where you want it.
• Clicking a region opens a panel: what the area is known for, the stones found there, and the mining tradition behind it.
• The selection carries straight into the sourcing form — the region arrives pre-filled, so the map is part of the enquiry.
• Smooth camera movement, subtle depth and considered motion — the interaction should feel like handling something valuable.
Why It Matters Commercially
Provenance is the single strongest selling point Sri Lankan gems have. Ratnapura is a name international buyers already recognise,
and a site that lets them choose it on a map is telling a story no competitor's dropdown can match.
It also does real commercial work: it educates the buyer, qualifies the enquiry before it reaches you, and gives visitors a reason to stay
on the page and to send the link to someone else.
Performance & Accessibility
Built with performance as a hard constraint: progressive loading, a lightweight fallback map on slow connections and low-power devices,
and full keyboard and screen-reader access to every region — so the experience is impressive without costing you mobile traffic or
search ranking.

07 SEO & DISCOVERABILITY
• Technical SEO from the ground up: clean URL structure, server-side rendering, sitemap, robots and structured data.
• Product schema markup on every stone page, so gems can surface in rich results with price and availability.
• Dedicated landing pages for the terms buyers actually search: Ceylon blue sapphire, Ratnapura sapphire, unheated padparadscha,
Sri Lankan gem sourcing.
• Page titles, meta descriptions and heading structure written for each key page rather than generated.
• Core Web Vitals treated as a build requirement, not an afterthought — image weight, script size and layout stability all budgeted.
• Google Analytics and Search Console connected and verified at launch.
Why Dedicated Pages Matter
International gem buyers search by stone and by origin, not by company name. A single catalogue page cannot rank for all of it.
Dedicated, well-written pages for each high-value search term give the site multiple independent entry points from Google, and each
one lands the buyer on a page built to convert them into a sourcing request.
